# MRR Quarterly Performance Analysis (2024)

Author: 24f1002781@ds.study.iitm.ac.in

## 📊 Overview

This analysis evaluates the Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) growth for 2024, using quarterly data:

| Quarter | MRR Growth |
|--------|------------|
| Q1     | 4.08       |
| Q2     | 6.77       |
| Q3     | 12.51      |
| Q4     | 13.36      |

**Average MRR Growth: 9.18**  
**Industry Target: 15**

## 📉 Key Findings

- MRR growth improved steadily across all four quarters.
- Despite strong Q3 and Q4 performance, the **annual average (9.18)** remains **below the industry target of 15**.
- The gap indicates that while momentum is strong, the business is not yet competitive with industry leaders.

## 📌 Business Implications

- Sub-target average growth may reduce competitive edge.
- Investment attractiveness may decrease unless acceleration continues.
- Strong late-year performance shows potential but lacks consistency.

## ✅ Recommendation

To reach the industry target of 15, the most impactful strategic action is:

### **➡️ Expand into new market segments**

This aligns with growth patterns observed and allows leveraging existing momentum for faster MRR scaling.
